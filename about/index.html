Clipr Website in short

Clipr Website rebuilds your website, every page you have today, from what you already have: your photos, your words and your colours, made better.

What you get
Your whole website in a new design: the homepage and all the other pages you have today. Designed for phones and computers, with your booking or contact details up front. Changes until you are happy with it. See Clipr Website
Price
$199 (£199 in the UK), paid once. The design concept is free to look at; you pay only when you say yes. No monthly fee to us.
Publishing
You get the finished website, ready to publish, with step-by-step help putting it live on your own domain. Your domain and hosting stay with your current provider.
What we do not do
We never log in to your website and never change it.
