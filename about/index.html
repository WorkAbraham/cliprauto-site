CliprAuto in short

CliprAuto is a done-for-you video clipping service. We turn long-form video such as podcasts, interviews, webinars and talks into finished short clips for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, LinkedIn and other platforms.

What it is
A service, not a software download. You send recordings. We deliver finished clips.
Who it is for
Podcasters, YouTube creators, founders, experts, educators and content teams that already record long-form video.
Input
A link (YouTube, Vimeo or Loom) or a video file.
Process
Transcribe the whole recording, find standalone moments, rate them, choose different non-overlapping moments, write an opening line, build each clip with captions, audio processing and a layout, run automated quality checks, then let the customer review the clips and ask for changes.
Output
Finished clips in 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 and 16:9, with a check report and a private review link.
Free sample
Yes. One episode, made into a sample from your own recording, without card or commitment.
Pricing
Monthly, paid by card or PayPal, cancel anytime from the next billing date: Starter $379, Growth $679, Premium $979 (USD per month). Details on the plans section of the home page.
What we do not do
We do not promise views, followers or sales. We do not sell software. We do not post on your accounts unless scheduling is included in your plan.
