What's in a sales pack?

Each sales pack covers one business and includes the following.

See a complete sample

  • Business research

    A summary of the business, its services, its location and its current website.

  • Website findings

    Website findings, clearly separated into verified facts and design assessments, with an explanation of how each was checked.

  • Website concept

    A new design concept built from the business's existing content, photos and branding.

  • Before-and-after image

    A visual comparison of the existing website and our proposed design, as an image file you can attach or embed.

  • Outreach email

    An email written for that specific business, ready for you to review and send.

  • Visit notification

    An email when activity is recorded on the preview link.

Questions about Pitch Engine

Is every sales pack made for a specific business?

Yes. We review that business's website and prepare the research, the design concept and the email from its own information.

Do you contact the businesses for me?

No. We prepare the material. Your agency decides who to contact, sends the email and handles any replies.

Will CliprAuto approach the businesses I send you?

No. We never contact a business your agency has sent us. Those businesses are yours.

Whose name is on the work?

The free sample pack is presented under the CliprAuto name, so you can judge our work. Sales packs you pay for can carry your agency's name and logo; you send us your logo and approve it once.

How long does a sales pack take?

Pilot sales packs are delivered by email within 24 hours of receiving the businesses you want us to work on. If we find them for you, the 24 hours start when you have approved the list.

What if something in a sales pack is wrong?

Tell us and we correct it. A finding we cannot back up is removed.

Who owns the material?

Once you have paid, your agency may use the material we create (the research, the concept, the images and the email) in your own outreach and proposals. Photos, logos and other material taken from the business's own website remain subject to their owners' rights. The free sample pack stays under the CliprAuto name.

Do I have to subscribe after the pilot?

No. The $99 pilot is a one-time payment. Production Partner is a separate monthly service you can choose if you want to continue. The pilot never turns into a subscription by itself.

Can I stop after the pilot or cancel Production Partner?

Yes. The pilot is one-time and never renews. Production Partner is our monthly service of up to 15 sales packs; you can cancel it in PayPal at any time. The subscription stops at the end of the month you have paid for.

What happens if a business wants to buy the website?

The client relationship is yours. If you want us to build the full website, you can order website production for $349 per site, white-labelled once you approve it.

Why does website production cost $349 when Clipr Website costs $199?

They are different services. Clipr Website is sold to the business itself, under the CliprAuto name. Website production is delivered to your agency: white-labelled once you approve it, and your agency handles the client relationship.

Do I need to use your hosting?

No. Hosting is optional and costs $19 a month per site.

Can you guarantee that a business will reply or buy?

No. We prepare the research, the design concept and the outreach material. Whether a business replies or buys depends on things outside our control.

What does a visit notification tell me?

That a visit to the preview link was recorded. It does not prove who opened it or whether they want to buy; email security scanners can also open links.
