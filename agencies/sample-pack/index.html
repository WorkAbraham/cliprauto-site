1. Research summary

Business
Alder & Finch Joinery (fictional). Family joinery: kitchens, wardrobes, stairs, doors and windows. Source: the original website. Tested at 390 px and 1440 px viewport widths.
What the site says
A family firm of joiners making kitchens, wardrobes, stairs, doors and windows for its local area. Enquiries come by phone. The site lists opening hours (Mon–Fri 08:00–16:00, Saturday by arrangement), a phone number, an email address and one photo.
Reason to approach
The website is laid out for larger screens, so on a phone it is shown zoomed out and is difficult to read. Asking for a quote means finding the number and phoning.
Suggested angle
Show how customers could see the services and get in touch more easily on a phone, using the business's own words.
Claims to avoid
Avoid claims about lost customers, revenue, search rankings or conversion rates. None of that has been measured.

2. Website findings

Each finding is marked as a checked fact or a design assessment. Only checked facts belong in the email.

  1. Contact Checked fact

    The phone number is plain text, not a tap-to-call link.

    How we checked: Read the page code at 390 px: 0 tel: links.

  2. Layout Checked fact

    The page is laid out at 980 px. On a 390 px phone screen it is shown zoomed out, with body text at about 5.6 px.

    How we checked: Measured the page width and text size at a 390 px viewport.

  3. Enquiries Checked fact

    There is no quote button; the page asks visitors to phone.

    How we checked: Looked for a quote button on the first screen at 390 px: none found.

  4. Overall look Design assessment

    Small serif text, underlined blue links and a visitor counter make the page look dated next to other local trades.

    How we checked: Viewed the page at 390 px and 1440 px. This is a design judgement, not a measurement.

  5. Outdated website information Design assessment

    The footer says the page was last updated in 2011 and is best viewed in Internet Explorer 6.

    How we checked: Read the footer text. This is page text, not proof of how customers see the business.

3. Website concept

Built from the business's own name, services, opening hours and contact details. No new business information or unverified claims have been added.
