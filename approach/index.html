How we work

We start with the website you already have.

We review the existing website, identify what can be improved and prepare a design concept using the business's own material.

Six principles

They apply to a single website and to an agency's sales packs.

  1. Look at what is already there

    We start with the existing website, its content and how it works on a phone and on a computer.

    Website and content review

  2. Find what needs attention

    We look for specific problems, such as a phone number you cannot tap or information that is hard to find on a phone.

    Each finding checked against the live page

  3. Prepare the work

    We use the business's own material to build the design concept and make the changes visible.

    Existing words, photos and colours

  4. Show you what we made

    You review the design or the sales material before you decide what happens next.

    Unlisted preview link

  5. Make the agreed changes

    For website customers, we make the agreed revisions before helping you get the site online.

    Changes until you are happy

  6. Keep the next step optional

    If you want more work from us, we tell you what it includes and what it costs before you commit.

    No automatic upgrades

Observation, change, check.

We explain what we found, what we changed and how we checked the result. We distinguish measurable facts from design assessments and don't make claims we cannot support.

We use automation for the repetitive parts: reading pages, measuring them on different screen sizes, putting a concept together. What gets shown to you is still based on that one business and its own material.

We do not
log in to your website, change anything on it without your yes, or invent reviews, prices or claims.
