Video clips for experts and companies: from one recording to a month of content

If you already record webinars, interviews or talks, the content exists. CliprAuto finds the moments in those recordings that make your expertise visible, and delivers them as finished, brand-consistent short clips. It takes work off your schedule instead of adding another tool.

Who this is for

Founders, consultants, educators, agencies and in-house content teams who record long-form video regularly and have a business reason to be visible online, but do not have time to cut, caption and format it all.

How a month looks

  1. You keep recording as usual. No new workflow.
  2. You send the recording link. One message per episode.
  3. We select and produce. Moments are chosen from the whole recording, built with captions, audio processing and your look, and checked by software before anyone sees them.
  4. You review and give feedback in one place. A private preview link. You pick looks and tell us what to change, and we repeat until you are happy.
  5. You receive ready-to-publish files in four formats. Scheduling on your accounts is included in the Premium plan.

What business buyers usually ask

  • Can the clips match our brand? Tell us your colour or caption style and we use it.
  • Is it private? Preview pages are private links, and we delete your link, address and sample after 30 days unless you become a client.
  • How do we pay? By card, PayPal or invoice, monthly. You pay on PayPal's secure page, never with card details typed into our site.
  • What do we get to see first? A free sample made from your own recording.

Questions and answers

Do you handle webinars and panel recordings?

Yes. Anything recorded as video that holds standalone moments: webinars, interviews, panels, talks and courses.

Can we ask for changes to the clips?

Yes. You get a private preview link, choose the look per clip and tell us what you want changed. We make the changes and you look again, as many times as it takes until you are happy.

How quickly do we get clips?

The 48 hours begin when your invoice is paid and we have your recording. The free sample is usually ready within a couple of hours.

Who owns the clips?

The clips are made from your recording and delivered to you as files.

What do you not do?

We do not promise views or followers, and we are not a software tool you operate. We are a service that delivers finished clips.
