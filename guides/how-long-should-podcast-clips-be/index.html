How long should podcast clips be?

By CliprAuto · Updated 2026-11-01

Short answer: as long as it takes to land one idea, and no longer. In our own work, finished clips usually run between about 20 and 40 seconds. The right length comes from the moment, not from a number.

A rule that works better than a target length

Find where the point starts and where it lands. Start on a whole sentence at the beginning of the idea. End on the sentence where the idea finishes, even if the speaker keeps talking. If you have to trim the middle to hit a length, the moment probably contains two ideas.

Rough guide by type of moment

Type of momentUsually works atWhy
A strong opinionShortThe claim and one reason are enough.
A practical explanationMediumIt needs a few steps to be useful.
A story with an endingMedium to longerIt needs setup and a conclusion to land.

This table is a rule of thumb from our own editing, not a platform rule.

Do not cut mid-sentence

A clip that ends in the middle of a sentence feels broken even if the length is perfect. Start and end on complete sentences.

Platform limits change

Maximum lengths on Reels, Shorts, TikTok and LinkedIn change over time, so check each platform's current rules before you plan around a limit. Most clips that stand on their own are well within them.

How to test your own

Play the clip to someone who has not heard the episode. If they can say what it was about, the length is right. If they are lost, it needs more context or a different moment. See how to choose moments to clip.

Questions and answers

What is the best length for a podcast clip?

There is no single best length. Aim for one complete idea starting and ending on whole sentences. Our finished clips usually run about 20 to 40 seconds.

Should I cut a clip shorter to hold attention?

Cut what is not needed, but not the context that makes the point understandable. A clip that needs a missing setup performs worse than one that is a few seconds longer.
