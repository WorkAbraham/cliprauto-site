Podcast clipping service: finished short clips from every episode

CliprAuto is a done-for-you clipping service for podcasts. You send a link to an episode. We transcribe it, find the moments that work on their own, and deliver finished vertical clips with captions, clean audio and a layout that fits your speakers. Every clip is checked before it reaches you.

Why good podcast clips are hard to make

Cutting a clip is easy. Choosing the right moment is the work. A moment has to make sense without the minutes before it, start and end on whole sentences, and leave the viewer with one clear idea. Most of the time in clipping goes into reading and listening through the episode, not into the cut itself.

How it works

  1. You send a link. A YouTube, Vimeo or Loom link, or the video file.
  2. We read the whole episode. The recording is transcribed and every possible standalone moment is rated for clarity, context and usefulness.
  3. We pick different moments. Clips come from different parts of the episode and cover different ideas, so you get variety instead of five versions of one point.
  4. We build and check each clip. Opening line, captions, framing, audio and layout. Software then checks the finished file: captions in sync, whole sentences, text inside the safe area, sound level and more.
  5. You review and give feedback. You get a private preview link, choose the look you like for each clip and tell us what to change. We change it and you look again until you are happy. Then you receive the clips in 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 and 16:9.

You start with a free sample made from your own episode, so you judge the quality before you pay.

Made for interview and conversation shows

Two-person conversations need layouts that follow who is speaking. Our looks include a full-frame view, a split view with host and guest stacked and the active speaker highlighted, a quote card over the voice, and big-word captions. You choose the look per clip.

What you get with every plan

  • Every clip in four picture formats: 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 and 16:9

Questions and answers

What is a podcast clipping service?

A service that takes your full episodes and turns the strongest moments into short, finished videos for social platforms. CliprAuto selects the moments, edits them with captions and audio processing, checks the result and delivers ready-to-publish files.

How many clips do I get from one episode?

That depends on your plan: Starter includes 12 clips a month from up to 2 source videos, Growth 30 clips from up to 6, Premium 50 clips from up to 10. There is no fixed number of good moments in an episode, so we choose what stands on its own rather than filling a quota.

Do you post the clips for me?

We deliver ready-to-publish files. Scheduling on your accounts is part of the Premium plan; on the other plans you publish the files yourself.

Does it work for audio-only podcasts?

We work from video recordings, such as YouTube, Vimeo or Loom links or video files. Audio-only shows need a video or visual version first.

Is the free sample really free?

Yes. You send one episode and get a sample made from it, with no card and no commitment.
