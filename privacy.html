Privacy

This page explains what information CliprAuto collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. CliprAuto is operated by Wiggle Entertainment AB (org.nr 559468-8904), Kalmar, Sweden, which is responsible for this information. Contact: info@cliprauto.com.

This website

Our main website does not use advertising cookies or third-party visitor analytics. The website concept form keeps a small counter in your browser's local storage to limit repeated attempts within an hour; it stays in your browser and is never sent to us.

Website concept requests

When you ask for a website concept, the form sends your website address and email address to our server. We use them to confirm your request, build your concept and email you about it. We read only the public pages of your website. We do this because you asked us to.

Agency work samples and enquiries

The work sample form opens an email to us with the details you entered. When you email us, we use your message and contact details to reply and to deliver what you asked for.

If we emailed your business

We sometimes contact businesses to offer our website redesign service. We prepare a free design concept using information from the business's existing website and email a link to it. There is no obligation to buy. This is done by Wiggle Entertainment AB (org.nr 559468-8904), Kalmar, Sweden, which is responsible for this information. Contact: info@cliprauto.com.

How to stop further marketing. Reply to our email and tell us you do not want to hear from us again. You can say "no thanks", "unsubscribe", or use your own words, and you do not need to give a reason. Once we receive your reply, we stop marketing to your business in all our channels, including any follow-up already planned. You can also write to info@cliprauto.com.

What information we use. Business names, website addresses, publicly listed business contact details or contact forms, and information published on the business's website, such as services, opening hours and photos. This may include personal information published in a business context, such as a contact person's name or work email address. We also keep records of our emails, your replies and any request not to be contacted again. We record when our preview emails are opened and when someone visits a concept through a preview link, as explained under Preview links.

Where it comes from. The business's own website, or a public business register or directory. You can ask us where we found your details.

Why we use it. To find businesses that may benefit from a new website, prepare a design concept, send our offer and respond to enquiries. Where this involves personal information, we rely on our legitimate interest in marketing our services to businesses. We limit the information we use to what is relevant to that purpose. You have the right to object to direct marketing at any time, free of charge.

Who handles it. one.com sends and receives our emails. Cloudflare hosts our website and preview pages. Anthropic provides AI models that help us write and design concepts. Cloudflare and Anthropic are based in the United States. Transfers to Cloudflare are covered by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and, where applicable, its UK extension, with standard contractual clauses as an alternative safeguard. We use Anthropic's Claude service to help prepare our design concepts; Anthropic processes submitted information under its consumer terms and privacy policy. You can contact us for more information about these safeguards.

How long we keep it. If we emailed your business and you have not replied or become a customer, we delete the design concept, our research files and your contact details 90 days after our last email, and we blank the subject and text of that email. This runs automatically every day. Before deleting, we keep a one-way scrambled fingerprint (a hash) of your email address on a do-not-contact list, so we never contact you again, and we keep the company's name and the date of contact without any address. You can also ask us to remove this sooner by writing to us. We may need to keep limited information to meet legal obligations. Preview visit records are deleted automatically after 14 days. If you ask us to stop contacting you, we keep coded versions of the email address, business and website domain on our do-not-contact list. We use them only to make sure we do not market to you again, and keep them for as long as that is needed. We keep this list because we must respect your objection.

Your other rights. You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold, ask us to correct it, delete it or limit its use, and object to its use. Write to info@cliprauto.com. If you think we have handled your information wrongly, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK (ico.org.uk) or the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (imy.se).

Preview links

Each design concept has an unlisted preview link: it is not published or indexed, but anyone with the link can open it. When a preview page is opened, or a preview email is opened, we record the time, the page, the country and city, the type of device, whether it looks like an automated visit, and a short one-way code made from the connection, which lets us count repeat visits but cannot be turned back into an IP address. We do not store the IP address itself. No cookies are set on preview pages. We use this to tell the business or agency that the preview was opened. These records are deleted automatically after 14 days.

Payments

Payments are made through PayPal. We receive the name, email address and payment details PayPal shares with us for the order, and use them to deliver what you bought and to keep the accounting records we are required to keep.

Why we may use this information

Website concept requests, enquiries and orders: to do what you asked us to do, before and after you buy. Payment and order records: to meet our accounting obligations. Reading public business websites, preparing concepts and recording preview activity: our legitimate interest in offering and following up our services to businesses, limited to business information and the records described above.

Service providers

Our server and preview pages run on Cloudflare and our emails are sent through Resend; they process information on our behalf. PayPal handles payments under its own privacy terms. Some of these providers may process information outside the EU/EEA; where they do, they rely on the transfer safeguards set out in their data protection terms, such as the EU standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep information

Preview visit records: 14 days. Requests and email conversations: as long as we need them to deliver and follow up on the request. Payment and order records: as long as accounting rules require.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to how we use it, or ask us to limit it. Write to info@cliprauto.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), imy.se.
