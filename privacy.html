Privacy

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. Visiting it tells us nothing about you. The sample form keeps a small counter in your browser to limit repeated attempts. It is never sent anywhere.

When you ask for a free sample, the form sends your video link and your email address to our server. We use them only to confirm your address, make your sample and reply. The server runs on Cloudflare and the confirmation emails are sent through Resend.

We delete your link, your address and your sample after 30 days unless you become a client. If you order a plan, the order form sends your name or company, email address, billing address and VAT number (if you give one) to the same server. We use them to send you the invoice and keep them for up to 13 months.

Questions? Write to info@cliprauto.com.
