Good to know

What exactly do I get for $199?

Your whole website in a new design, every page you have today: the design, the layout, your text tightened, your photos placed, working on a phone and on a computer, with your contact details up front. Delivered ready to publish on your domain, with step-by-step help.

Built-in features such as online shops, member logins, payments or booking tools that run inside your current site are checked with you before you decide to buy.

Is there a monthly fee?

No monthly fee from us. You pay $199 once, or £199 if you are a UK customer. That is the final price, with nothing added on top. Domain and hosting costs are paid separately to your chosen provider. Depending on your current website platform, you may need different hosting.

What happens after I send my website address?

You get an email asking you to confirm your request. After you confirm, we prepare your design concept and email you a private link to it. You do not need to book a call or pay anything to see it. If you have not received your link, write to info@cliprauto.com and we will tell you where your request stands.

When do I pay?

Only after you have seen your design concept and said yes. The payment link comes by email, and you pay through PayPal.

Do I need to book a call?

No. You send your address, we send a preview, you reply by email.

What if I don't like the design?

Then you pay nothing. Looking is free.

What if you can't deliver what we agreed?

Then you get a full refund.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Tell us what to change and we adjust it. Changes until you are happy are part of the price.

I have no website yet. Can you still help?

Yes. Write to info@cliprauto.com with a few photos and a few lines about your business, and we build the concept from that.

How does my new website go live?

We help you, step by step: you get the finished website ready to publish, and we show you how to put it live on your own domain.

If your current site is built with Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, Weebly, Jimdo, GoDaddy Website Builder or Webnode, the new website cannot be uploaded there. You keep your domain, and publishing needs a hosting service that accepts an uploaded website.

Will you change my existing website?

No. We only read the public pages a visitor can open. Nothing on your site changes until you say yes.

Will I have any domain or hosting costs?

You'll still need a domain and compatible web hosting. If your current provider supports the new website, you may be able to keep it. Otherwise, we'll help you find a suitable hosting service.

Will my booking system still work?

We aim to keep your existing booking system. If it uses an external booking link, we can retain that link. If it is built into your current website, we'll check compatibility before you decide to buy.

Who owns the finished website?

You do. Once you have paid, the website files we made for you (the pages, the layout and the code) are yours to publish, change and keep. Your own photos, logo and text remain yours, as they always were.
