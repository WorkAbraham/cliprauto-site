What we check

  1. MobileFits a phone screen without sideways scrolling, text you can read, buttons big enough to tap, and your main action visible without scrolling.
  2. Search and sharingA page title and description, one main heading, descriptions on your images, a sitemap, and a preview when your link is shared.
  3. SpeedHow big the page is, how long it takes to load, how many files it pulls in, and images that are far larger than they need to be.
  4. Trust and contactA secure connection, contact details a visitor can find, links that lead nowhere, and content that the browser blocks as unsafe.
  5. TechnicalPage language, an icon in the browser tab, errors in the browser console and a clear main address for the page.

Questions about Clipr Website

Is it really free?

Yes, during Beta. You enter your address and your email and get a private report. No card, no call.

What do you look at?

Only the public pages a visitor can open: your front page and a few of the pages it links to. We check them on a phone-sized screen and a computer-sized screen. We never log in, and we change nothing on your site.

Who writes the report?

It is automatic. Every finding comes from something we measured on your pages, and the text explains why it matters and how to fix it. Nothing is guessed about your business.

How long does it take?

Usually a few minutes once you have confirmed your email. If many people ask at once, you wait in line and we email you when it is ready.

Who can see my report?

Only people who have the link. The page is private, not listed anywhere, and is deleted after 30 days.

Can you fix what you find?

Write to info@cliprauto.com and tell us which points you want fixed. We reply with what it would take.

Why only a few pages?

To keep the review fast and polite to your server. If your site blocks automatic visitors, the review may fail and we tell you so.
