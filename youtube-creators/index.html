Turn your long YouTube videos into Shorts, Reels and TikToks

Your long videos already contain short ones. CliprAuto reads a long video, finds the parts that stand on their own, and delivers them as finished vertical clips with captions and a look you choose. You send a link. We do the rest.

The problem with doing it yourself

Making Shorts from long videos sounds simple until you do it every week: scrub through the video, decide what works without context, cut cleanly on whole sentences, caption it, check the framing and export in the right sizes. It is hours of work that comes after you have already finished the real video.

What we do differently

  • Different moments, not copies. Each clip comes from a different part of the video and carries its own idea.
  • Whole sentences. Clips begin and end on complete sentences.
  • Captions that match the speech. Word by word, checked against the audio.
  • Your choice of look. Full frame, big words, quote card or other layouts. You pick per clip.
  • Every format. 9:16 for Shorts, Reels and TikTok, plus 4:5, 1:1 and 16:9.

Questions and answers

Do you change my original video?

No. Your original stays as it is. We make new short clips from it.

Can I send a YouTube link?

Yes. YouTube, Vimeo and Loom links work, and you can also send the video file.

What if my video already has captions burned in?

Captions that are part of the picture can collide with ours and can be cut off when we reframe to vertical. Our builder detects this, so a clean version without burned-in text gives the best result.

How is this different from an AI clip tool?

A tool hands you files. We select moments with an editorial check, build each clip with our own editing engine, run automated quality checks on every file and deliver it as a service you do not have to operate.

Do you guarantee views?

No. We choose moments that stand on their own and make sure they are well made. How your audience responds is up to the audience.
